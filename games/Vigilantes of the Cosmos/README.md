# Vigilantes of the Cosmos

**Team Members:** Jack Boland, Cassandra Boland

**How would you pitch your game in one line?**
Sidescrolling space shooter taking inspiration from Black Paradox, Ikaruga, etc. Co-op shooter with planet traversal as map navigation and rpg elements.

**What is the key mechanic(s) that make it fun?**
Co-op, RPG ship customization, Navigable Galaxy, Dead or Alive system.

**How does the game actually play? (Core Game Loop)**
Players will move from planet to planet defending them from an unknown threat that may change with each new location. 
When the player has lowered the health of the boss enough, they can ram the ship to board them by shooting through their windows and arrest them on the spot for extra rewards.
After defeating the threat, players will be able to select weapons, armor upgrades, powerups, and more before heading into the next zone.

**How will you keep players engaged for 30 minutes? What's the general scope of your game (will change as you go)?**
Players will start by learning how to play through a tutorial level that helps learn the core moveset and one shopping phase where they will then be able to select a new
planet to travel to and begin their journey.
